Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026 · {{ app_name }} ({{ site_host }})

{{ app_name }} (“we”, “the Service”) helps Sri Lankan businesses process supplier invoices and plan cheque payments. This policy explains what data we handle when you use the web app or send invoice photos via WhatsApp connected to this instance.

Data we process

  • Account access: a shop login password for the web dashboard.
  • Business records: invoice images, extracted invoice fields, dealer/supplier details, bank account labels, cheque plans, and related cash-flow notes you enter or approve.
  • WhatsApp: if WhatsApp is connected, we receive message metadata and media (for example invoice photos) from numbers that message the linked business WhatsApp account, including the sender’s phone number.
  • Technical logs: server logs needed to operate and secure the Service (for example webhook delivery and error messages).

How we use data

  • To show invoice photos in the shop dashboard and run OCR / verification workflows you trigger.
  • To prepare cheque bundling, printing, and cash-flow planning for the shop that operates this instance.
  • To reply on WhatsApp when the configuration allows outbound messages.
  • To maintain security (for example ignoring messages from numbers not on an allow-list when one is configured).

AI providers

When the shop enables AI features, invoice images or text may be sent to third-party AI providers (for example Google Gemini for document reading, and optionally OpenAI for chat assistants) solely to provide those features. Those providers process data under their own terms and policies.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by hosting providers (for example the cloud host running this instance) and by Meta when WhatsApp Cloud API is used. Access is limited to the shop operators of this {{ app_name }} instance and the systems they configure.

Retention

Invoice images and business records are kept until the shop deletes them or the hosting database is reset. On free-tier hosts without persistent storage, data may be lost when the service restarts.

Your choices

  • Shop operators can dismiss WhatsApp inbox items and manage allow-listed sender numbers in settings.
  • You may request deletion of personal data associated with your WhatsApp number; see our Data deletion page.
